What you are looking at. Stock SWMM routes kinematic wave and steady flow in one upstream-to-downstream pass, so it refuses a junction with two outlets (ERROR 133) and a network where water can come back around (ERROR 131). swmm5-kw is the same EPA 5.2.4 engine with those two refusals replaced by an allocation rule and a per-step iteration.

When there is nothing to split or loop, swmm5-kw writes the same bytes as stock. The Run tab shows that live: the two .out fingerprints match.

The contract

        
    
          
  1. Off means stock. With both options off (the engine's default) the bytes are stock 5.2.4, ERROR 133 and 131 included.
    2. 
          
  2. On changes nothing it has no business changing. A network with nothing to split or loop gives the same bytes as stock in every mode.
    3. 
          
  3. No order dependence. Shuffling the file's junctions and conduits changes nothing, and neither does where an outlet sits in the file. Engine v1 broke this at junctions with more than 64 outlets under CAPACITY and AREA; v1.1 fixes it (Doc tab).
    4. 
          
  4. Conservation. A junction hands out exactly what arrived, up to its outlets' combined capacity; a loop's leftover is booked to the receiving node. In engine v1, HEAD could hand out up to 1.5e-6 cfs more than arrived (20 corpus networks); v1.1, the engine in this page, fixes it (Doc tab).
    5. 
          
  5. One engine, one answer. The wasm engine matches a native build of the same source and the same maths library byte for byte.
    6.

About swmm5-kw

        
swmm5-kw is EPA SWMM 5.2.4 with one change in purpose: kinematic wave and steady flow routing no longer stop at a junction with several outlets (ERROR 133) or at a directed loop (ERROR 131). A split junction shares its outflow among its outlets by one of three rules; a loop is iterated within each routing step until the flow coming back around settles. Dynamic wave is untouched, and with both options off the engine is stock 5.2.4.

        
This page runs two engines compiled to WebAssembly from the same source tree: stock EPA SWMM 5.2.4 from the official v5.2.4 tag, and the same tree with the extension. The source, the patch, the test networks, the gates and their logs are in the repository SWMMBobSWMM6/swmm5-kw, with the Doc essay as a Word file (dist/swmm5-kw-doc.docx).

        
The EPA Storm Water Management Model is a work of the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency, in the public domain. Version 5.2.4's engine is Lewis Rossman's design.

        
A modified engine. Results from swmm5-kw with either option on are not stock EPA SWMM results. Its report says so in a KW/SF extension block; keep that block with any result you pass on.

What changed in 1.1

        
An outside review of v1.0 (31 October 2026) found a defect in the engine and five ways this page could misreport a run. Each was reproduced first, then fixed, and each now has a check that fails on v1.0:

        
    
          
  1. Engine, 65 outlets. CAPACITY and AREA kept their "outlet is full" flags in char done[64], so a capped outlet past the 64th was never set aside and the junction flooded. Now one flag per outlet, no limit. Two gates cover it (Verify tab).
    2. 
          
  2. Engine, HEAD. Found by the corpus pass the same day: HEAD could hand out up to 1.5e-6 cfs more than arrived. Fixed in the same release, on Bob's decision.
    3. 
          
  3. Stale results. Results now carry the run that made them, say when the inputs have changed, and export under the run's own model name.
    4. 
          
  4. Stopped runs. A run that did not finish gets a "Stopped" verdict saying how far it got, and no comparison or export.
    5. 
          
  5. Peaks. Missing values export blank, never -Infinity; every peak keeps its sign, with maximum, minimum and a backwards-flow flag.
    6. 
          
  6. Time axis. Each point sits at the time stamped on it in the .out file; the first hourly sample is at hour 1.
    7. 
        

        
A second session fixed the same review in parallel on branch claude/kw-v1.1-review-fixes; its fan-out gate, browser checks, outlet census and two figures are folded into this version.

On 31 October 2026 it reproduced each finding of an outside review, fixed the two allocation defects in the engine (v1.1, on Bob's decision) and the five ways this page could misreport a run (stale results after a model change, a stopped run shown as a result, missing values exported as infinities, reversed flows shown as zero, and a hydrograph drawn one report step early), and added gates for them.

Byte comparison of .out and .rpt files against stock 5.2.4 built from the official tag, on the shipped networks and on Bob's corpus; the wasm engines against native builds; the patch against a fresh checkout. Not yet checked by a person: the C changes have not been reviewed line by line by Bob, and the split rules have been compared with dynamic wave on five small test networks only.

Kinematic wave, now with forks in the road

    
Stock SWMM's kinematic wave and steady flow need a tree: one way out of every junction, no way back. swmm5-kw lets the water fork and come round again, and leaves everything else byte for byte as EPA wrote it.
