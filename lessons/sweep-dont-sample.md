# Sweep, don't sample

**What happened.** Stock 5.2.4's kinematic-wave continuity was measured on a three-pipe chain at
30, 34 and 40 cfs in September: 0.124%, −1.596%, −0.064%. The −1.6% was reported as the problem.
Sweeping the inflow in 0.5 cfs steps from 20 to 46 cfs found the error growing to −10.18% at
37.5 cfs and collapsing to −0.48% at 38. Three points had stepped over the whole band.

**The rule.** When a behaviour changes near a threshold (pipe capacity here), sweep through the
threshold finely before quoting a number. A sweep of 53 kinematic-wave runs on a three-pipe chain
took two seconds.
